How will you print it?
How to print on Avery sheets
  1. Buy Avery 8371 business card sheets, or sheets with exactly the same layout.
  2. Open the PDF and print it on plain paper first. Hold it against a card sheet to the light to check that the cards line up.
  3. In the print settings, set the scale to Actual size or 100%. Never choose “Fit to page”.
  4. Load the card sheet and print.
How to order from a print shop
  1. Send the PDF to your print shop as it is. It already has 3 mm bleed, crop marks and print colours (CMYK).
  2. No fonts are needed: all text is saved as outlines.
  3. Ask for a proof before the full run.

Before printing, consider going digital. The eBusiness Card app makes a card with a QR code. When someone scans it, your contact details go straight into their phone.

  1. Open the app and sign in with your UN account.
  2. Fill in your details and create your card.
  3. You get an image of the card with its QR code by email. Save it on your phone to share it.

The app can also scan printed business cards and save the contact straight into Outlook.
